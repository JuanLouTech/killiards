How to play

  • 🏆 Last ball alive wins. Take turns shooting your ball like in billiards.
  • 🎯 On your turn, drag anywhere on the pad and release — slingshot style: pull back, shoot forward. The arrow shows direction and power. You have 60 seconds: when the clock runs out, the shot fires by itself!
  • 🌀 Tap SPIN to move the hit point. It changes how your ball behaves on its first contact: high = follow through, low = pull back, sides = kick toward that side.
  • 💥 Ramming other balls damages them, proportionally to the impact. Your own ball is immune to ball hits during your turn.
  • ⚡ Every new contact with a border or obstacle costs a fixed chunk of energy — including yours, so aim carefully! The host picks how much in the lobby (none / low / high).
  • 💀 Depleted balls explode and stay as heavy dead balls: they take no damage, but you can shove them into enemies.
  • 🎁 Power-ups appear on the table for a few turns, and whichever ball touches one takes it. 💥⚡ store and fire on that player's next shot. 💚☠️ heal or hurt instantly. 🐜🪨 make the ball tiny or heavy for the whole next turn — check who plays next and shove your enemies wisely!
  • 🌀 Some tables have teleporters (matching rings) and pushable barriers (blue squares) you can launch at people.
  • 🤖 The host can add up to 3 bots (yellow ring) in the lobby, and tap their chip to set the difficulty: EASY / MID / HARD. Hard bots simulate their shots — watch out.
  • 🏆 The host can turn the game into a tournament of 3, 5 or 8 matches: a random table every match, the top four score 6 / 4 / 2 / 1 points, and the highest total takes the crown.
  • 💬 Tap the chat button to talk. New messages pop up briefly — open the chat to read them all.
  • 🎲 Tables have different obstacles; the host picks one (or rolls Random) before the match.
