How to play

  • 🏆 Last ball alive wins. Take turns shooting your ball like in billiards.
  • 🎯 On your turn, drag anywhere on the pad and release — slingshot style: pull back, shoot forward. The arrow shows direction and power.
  • 🌀 Tap SPIN to move the hit point. It changes how your ball behaves on its first contact: high = follow through, low = pull back, sides = curve away.
  • 💥 Ramming other balls damages them, proportionally to the impact. Your own ball is immune to ball hits during your turn.
  • ⚡ Every new contact with a border or obstacle costs a fixed chunk of energy — including yours, so aim carefully!
  • 💀 Depleted balls explode and stay as heavy dead balls: they take no damage, but you can shove them into enemies.
  • 🎲 Tables have different obstacles; the host picks one (or rolls Random) before the match.
